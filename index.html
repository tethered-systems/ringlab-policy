RingLab privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

RingLab is a netball coaching app for Android, published by tethered.systems (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your videos and results stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google’s advertising service needs to show ads, and what Google Play needs to process a purchase.

What stays on your phone

  • Videos you record or import, and the body-position data (a “skeleton”) worked out from them.
  • Scores, faults, cues, goal and miss labels, notes, drill logs, umpiring quiz results, game scores and settings, including the player name you choose to enter.

Video analysis runs entirely on your phone using an on-device machine-learning model (Google MediaPipe). Its usage-statistics reporting has been removed from RingLab. None of this information is uploaded to us or anyone else. We do not run any servers and we never receive your videos, results or settings. RingLab does not create an account and does not back your data up to the cloud.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you record a shooting set. RingLab never records sound. Importing an existing clip uses Android’s photo picker and needs no permission.
  • Notifications: to show analysis progress, and practice reminders if you turn them on.
  • Internet: only for ads, ad consent, and the “Remove ads” purchase.

Advertising

RingLab shows ads using Google AdMob. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as your phone’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location;
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information about ad performance.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, RingLab asks for your consent through Google’s consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices. Ads in RingLab are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rating PG). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Android settings. Google’s use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

No ads are requested, and no advertising data is collected, while “Player is under 16” is turned on or after you buy “Remove ads”.

Purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play under Google Payments’ privacy notice. We do not see your payment details. RingLab keeps a record on your phone that ads have been removed, and checks with Google Play when the app starts.

Children

RingLab is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Parents and coaches filming a player should have the player’s, or their parent’s, permission. Turning on “Player is under 16” switches off all ads.

Keeping or deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a single session, delete videos older than 90 days automatically, or delete everything in Settings → Your data. Uninstalling RingLab deletes all of it. Because we hold none of your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your information is stored in RingLab’s private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Advertising and purchase traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
